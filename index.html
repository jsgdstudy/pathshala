Who We Are

About Our Pathshala

Since 1975, the Jain Society of Greater Detroit has been a thriving spiritual and cultural center, supporting the Jain community in Detroit and nearby areas. Established by dedicated members from Michigan and Canada, the society has grown to support over 600 families across multiple counties. The Society formalized its Articles of Organization in 1981.

🎯 Our Mission

To share the timeless teachings of Jainism, fostering both spiritual and educational growth and championing charitable initiatives. We aim to make Jain wisdom accessible to individuals from all backgrounds, promoting inner peace and encouraging positive societal contributions.

🌍 Our Vision

Through Jain scriptures, spiritual practices, and community service, we envision a world of harmony where individuals live with awareness, joy, and compassion.

Our Pathshala

Classes & Community

📅 Class Schedule

Classes meet every Sunday during the academic year. Timing and location details are shared with registered families at the start of each semester.

👩‍👧 Who Can Join

Open to children ages 5–18. We welcome all families interested in Jain education regardless of prior background. Classes are grouped by age for the best learning experience.

📚 What We Teach

Jain prayers & aarti, Jain history & tirthankars, values & ethics, Prakrit & Sanskrit basics, arts & crafts with Jain themes, and cultural celebrations.

🤝 Community

More than a classroom — our Pathshala is a place where families connect, celebrate festivals together, and build lifelong friendships rooted in Jain values.
