Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To share the timeless teachings of Jainism, fostering both spiritual and educational growth and championing charitable initiatives. We aim to make Jain wisdom accessible to individuals from all backgrounds, promoting inner peace and encouraging positive societal contributions.

🌍 Our Vision

Through Jain scriptures, spiritual practices, and community service, we envision a world of harmony where individuals live with awareness, joy, and compassion.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🕊️ Ahimsa

Non-violence in thought, word, and deed — the foundation of Jain ethics and the highest virtue.

✅ Satya

Truthfulness and honesty in all aspects of life, speaking only what is true, beneficial, and kind.

🙏 Asteya

Non-stealing; taking only what is rightfully given and not coveting what belongs to others.

🧘 Brahmacharya

Chastity and self-discipline — restraint of the senses and channeling energy toward spiritual growth.

🌿 Aparigraha

Non-possessiveness and simplicity — limiting attachment to material things and living with contentment.

Learning

Classes

📅 Schedule

Pathshala classes meet every Sunday during the academic year. Timing and location details are shared with registered families at the start of each semester.

📚 What We Teach

Our curriculum covers Jain prayers & aarti, Jain history & tirthankars, ethics & values, Prakrit & Sanskrit basics, and arts & crafts with Jain themes. We have 8 groups (G1–G8) structured to provide age-appropriate learning for children from beginners through advanced levels.

👧 Who Can Join

Open to children of all backgrounds. All families interested in Jain education are welcome regardless of prior experience.

Together

Community

🤝 More Than a Classroom

Our Pathshala is a place where families connect, celebrate festivals together, and build lifelong friendships rooted in Jain values. Open to children ages 5–18; all families are welcome regardless of prior background.

🎉 Events & Celebrations

We host cultural events, Paryushan celebrations, Diwali programs, and volunteer opportunities throughout the year that bring the entire community together.
